# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a friendly, welcoming place for everyone, whatever their experience, background, identity or level of skill. Designers, developers and site owners who have never opened a terminal are all welcome here.

## What we expect

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new to Framer, Claude or PageSpeed.
* Give feedback on the work, not the person.
* Assume good intent, and ask before you assume.
* Accept constructive criticism with grace, and say thanks when someone helps.
* Respect privacy. Don't post someone else's site, client details or personal information without permission.

## What is not acceptable

* Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks
* Sexualized language or imagery
* Discriminatory jokes or comments
* Publishing anyone's private information without their permission
* Spam, self promotion unrelated to the project, or repeated off topic posts
* Any other behavior that would be unwelcome in a professional setting

## Where this applies

In this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior worries you, email **hello@studiohans.agency**. Every report is read and handled privately. We'll respond as quickly as we can.

## What happens next

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break this code of conduct. Depending on what happened, that can range from a private note, to a public warning, to a temporary or permanent ban.
